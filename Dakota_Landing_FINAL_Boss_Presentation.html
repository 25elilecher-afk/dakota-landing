The Course

A quick local round, done right.

Dakota Landing opened in 1998 and is a public 9-hole layout designed by Jim Baumberger and Steve Rentz. From the back tees it plays about 2,795 yards, with rolling terrain, trees, water hazards and a memorable island-green feature.
